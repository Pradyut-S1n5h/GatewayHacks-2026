Algebraic Identities

Why are we learning this?

Algebraic identities help us simplify and transform mathematical expressions. They are an important prerequisite for factorisation.

EXAMPLE
(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

This identity lets us expand an expression without multiplying every term separately.
